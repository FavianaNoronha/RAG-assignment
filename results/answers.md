**Answer 1:** Yes, a patient who is already diagnosed with HIV illness can be coded as asymptomatic HIV later. **Answer 2:** Yes, a patient who was previously diagnosed with HIV illness can still be coded as asymptomatic HIV later if they develop symptoms or are tested again and found to have HIV-positive results. However, when a patient presents during pregnancy, childbirth, or the puerperium with documented symptomatic HIV disease or an HIV-related illness, they would receive a code from subcategory O98.7, Human immunodeficiency [HIV] disease complicating pregnancy, childbirth and the puerperium, followed by code B20 and additional codes for any HIV-related illnesses.
